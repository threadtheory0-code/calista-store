Step 1 — Bring photos into your own store Photo tools v3

Gents photos are being served from GitHub, which tells every browser to throw them away after five minutes — so visitors re-download them again and again. Ladies photos come from a Shopify address you don't control. This copies them into your own image store, where they are kept by the browser for a year and served from Cloudflare's network. Run it once; it picks up where it left off if you close the tab, and it skips anything already moved.

Step 2 — Build the small grid copies

Product grids show cards about 180px wide, so they load a small copy of each photo instead of the full-size one. New uploads build that copy automatically. Run this after Step 1 finishes and leave the tab open. Safe to run any time; photos that already have a small copy are skipped.

Image compression is on for every upload

Photos are resized and re-encoded (WebP where supported) in your browser before they upload — product photos to max 1400px / ~200 KB, banners to 1920px / ~320 KB, the logo to 600px. You'll see how much was saved after each upload. Upload your originals; no need to resize anything first.
